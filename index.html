Features

📊

Structured Workouts

Library of 140+ professional .zwo format workouts with visual power charts and zone-based training

📁

Custom Workout Import

Import your own .zwo files. Find workouts at What's On Zwift or learn to create your own

💓

HR Zone Control

PID-based algorithm automatically adjusts trainer resistance to maintain your heart rate in the target zone. Real-time Power & HR visualization with zone tracking.

⚡

FTP-Based Training

Power-based workouts calibrated to your Functional Threshold Power

📈

Real-Time Metrics

Live power, cadence, speed, and heart rate monitoring during workouts

💾

Workout Recording

Track your sessions with detailed history and performance charts. Direct upload to Strava or export TCX files.

🎯

BIAS Control

Adjust workout intensity on-the-fly with mid-workout FTP scaling

✏️

Workout Customization

Easily customize any workout from the 140+ workout library or your imported workouts. Adjust segment durations, power targets, and intervals to match your training needs.

Built by a Rider, For Riders

I've been cycling for 12 years, and three years ago I started adding indoor trainer sessions to my weekly outdoor rides. Like many cyclists, I subscribed to Zwift—but three years later, I realized I was paying $240 a year for a virtual world I never looked at. I wanted the essential training features without the bloat: smart trainer control, heart rate zone training, structured workouts, and real-time metrics. That's it.

RideByHeart is the app I wish existed when I started indoor training. It's currently free during the initial launch phase. When pricing launches, it's expected to be a one-time purchase, not a subscription. No features you'll never use. Just you, your trainer, and focused training sessions that respect both your time and your wallet.

I've canceled my Zwift subscription and now use RideByHeart for all my indoor sessions. This is version 2.2—not perfect, but it works. I'm releasing it now to get feedback and iterate quickly.
